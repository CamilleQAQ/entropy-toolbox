# Scientific references

The Python source in this repository was written independently except for the
four CC BY 4.0 adaptations identified in
[`provenance.md`](provenance.md). The references below acknowledge the
scientific origin of the implemented methods; citation does not imply that a
paper's authors wrote or endorsed this software.

Users should cite the papers relevant to the methods they use.

## General multiscale and entropy methods

- M. Costa, A. L. Goldberger, and C.-K. Peng, “Multiscale Entropy Analysis of
  Complex Physiologic Time Series,” *Physical Review Letters*, 89, 068102,
  2002. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.89.068102
- J. S. Richman and J. R. Moorman, “Physiological Time-Series Analysis Using
  Approximate Entropy and Sample Entropy,” *American Journal of
  Physiology—Heart and Circulatory Physiology*, 278(6), H2039–H2049, 2000.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpheart.2000.278.6.H2039
- S.-D. Wu, C.-W. Wu, S.-G. Lin, K.-Y. Lee, and C.-K. Peng, “Analysis of
  Complex Time Series Using Refined Composite Multiscale Entropy,” *Physics
  Letters A*, 378(20), 1369–1374, 2014.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physleta.2014.03.034

## Permutation and slope entropy

- C. Bandt and B. Pompe, “Permutation Entropy: A Natural Complexity Measure
  for Time Series,” *Physical Review Letters*, 88, 174102, 2002.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.174102
- D. Cuesta-Frau, “Slope Entropy: A New Time Series Complexity Estimator Based
  on Both Symbolic Patterns and Amplitude Information,” *Entropy*, 21(12),
  1167, 2019. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/e21121167

## Dispersion and fuzzy dispersion entropy

- M. Rostaghi and H. Azami, “Dispersion Entropy: A Measure for Time-Series
  Analysis,” *IEEE Signal Processing Letters*, 23(5), 610–614, 2016.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/LSP.2016.2542881
- H. Azami, M. Rostaghi, D. Abásolo, and J. Escudero, “Refined Composite
  Multiscale Dispersion Entropy and Its Application to Biomedical Signals,”
  *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, 64(12), 2872–2879, 2017.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2017.2679136
- M. Rostaghi, M. M. Khatibi, M. R. Ashory, and H. Azami, “Fuzzy Dispersion
  Entropy: A Nonlinear Measure for Signal Analysis,” *IEEE Transactions on
  Fuzzy Systems*, 30(9), 3785–3796, 2022.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TFUZZ.2021.3128957
- M. Rostaghi, M. M. Khatibi, M. R. Ashory, and H. Azami, “Refined
  Composite Multiscale Fuzzy Dispersion Entropy and Its Applications to
  Bearing Fault Diagnosis,” *Entropy*, 25(11), 1494, 2023.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/e25111494
- H. Azami and J. Escudero, “Refined Multiscale Fuzzy Entropy Based on
  Standard Deviation for Biomedical Signal Analysis,” *Medical & Biological
  Engineering & Computing*, 55, 2037–2052, 2017.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11517-016-1607-0
- H. Azami, S. Sanei, and T. K. Rajji, “Ensemble Entropy: A Low Bias
  Approach for Data Analysis,” *Knowledge-Based Systems*, 256, 109876,
  2022. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2022.109876

- T. D. Pham, “Time-Shift Multiscale Entropy Analysis of Physiological

  DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/e19060257
